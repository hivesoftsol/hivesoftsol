Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Your App Name can remove their data and revoke access to their Google account.

Does the app store user accounts?

Your App Name does not maintain developer-managed user accounts. Users sign in with their Google account only to authorize access to their own Google Drive for backup and restore functionality.

How to stop using the app

  1. Open the app and stop using the backup feature.
  2. Delete any backup files stored in your Google Drive.
  3. Uninstall the application if you no longer wish to use it.

How to revoke Google Drive access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time by visiting:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Locate Your App Name and choose Remove Access.

Data deletion

The application does not store your backup files or personal data on developer-owned servers. Backup files are stored only in your own Google Drive account.

To permanently delete your backup data, simply delete the backup files from your Google Drive.

Data retained

The developer does not retain copies of your backup files after Google Drive access has been revoked. Some anonymous analytics information may be processed through Firebase Analytics in accordance with Google's policies.
