Delete Your Account

Your privacy is important to us. If you no longer wish to use Smart Printer, you can permanently delete your account directly from the app. Please follow the steps below.

How to Delete Your Account

1
Open Your Profile

Navigate to My Profile and scroll to the bottom of the page. Tap the Delete Account button.

2
Confirm Deletion

A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap Delete Account again to permanently remove your account and all associated data.

⚠ Important
  • Your account will be permanently deleted.
  • Your fax history will be removed.
  • Your purchased tokens will be deleted.
  • Your personal fax number (if assigned) will be removed.
  • This action cannot be undone.
